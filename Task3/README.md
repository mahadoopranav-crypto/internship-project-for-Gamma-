## Report

**1. Which 10 companies publish the most job listings?**
IQ-EQ Mauritius leads with 17 listings, followed by Le Jadis Beach Resort and Wellness (10), New Mauritius Hotels (Beachcomber) (8), Ninety Six Hotel Collection (7) and Shanti Maurice Resort & Spa (6). Six companies tie for the remaining places with 5 listings each: Blue Connect, CIEL Textile Group, Groupe Beau Vallon, Linkeo, Proactive Talent Solutions and Swan Life, so the chart shows 11 companies. Five of the top companies are hotel groups. Together these 11 employers posted 78 listings (26%), out of 152 employers in the data.

**2. Which job categories and locations have the highest number of listings?**
Accounting / Auditing / Tax Services / Finance is the largest category with 67 listings (22.3%), followed by Tourism / Travel (41, 13.7%) and ICT / IT / Web (29, 9.7%). By location, Plaines Wilhems (101, 33.7%) and Port Louis (72, 24.0%) together hold 57.7% of all listings, with Pamplemousses third (37, 12.3%).

**3. How many listings close within the next seven days, by category?**
21 listings (7%) close between 24 and 31 October 2026. ICT / IT / Web and Healthcare / Medical / Veterinary have the most, with 3 each. Administrative / Clerical, Manufacturing / Production, Insurance and Call Centers / BPO / Customer Service / Languages have 2 each, and seven other categories have 1 each. Healthcare stands out: 3 of its 4 listings close this week.
